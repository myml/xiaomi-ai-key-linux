# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful. Disagreement about a technical decision is fine; personal
  attacks, harassment, and discrimination are not.
- Assume good faith. Many contributors here are debugging their own laptop at
  midnight; a short, kind answer costs nothing.
- Keep issues and pull requests about the code and the problem. Report
  off-topic or hostile behaviour to the maintainers instead of replying in
  kind.
- English or Chinese are both welcome in issues and pull requests.

Maintainers may edit, lock, or remove comments and contributions that violate
this code, and may ban repeat offenders. To report something, open a private
security advisory or contact the maintainer listed in `SECURITY.md`.
